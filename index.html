Being celiac in a land of parathas

Celiac disease means even a crumb of gluten is a no. That's tough in a country where wheat turns up in everything from rotis to the hing in your dal.

I've lived with it since 1991, so I've asked every question a waiter can be asked. Here I share what's actually safe to eat out in India, what I cook at home, and memes for when it all gets a bit much.
